LATTICE RIFT

Seven game screens, exploded into fractals and put back together wrong. Then you break them.

Lattice Rift: seven game interfaces, exploded into GLSL fractals, which the keyboard melts, a gamepad tears, the space bar turns to molecules and a drag draws strands across

any key melts the page where that key sits on the keyboard. ← → tear it down the middle, ↑ ↓ across; a gamepad’s stick and d-pad tear too, its face buttons make molecules and its shoulders melt. Hold space for molecules. Drag anywhere that is not text to draw strands. The Mend button puts it back.

Each console renders both screens at 256×192, the DS resolution, into textures. The page’s stage composites them with each section’s background, then runs three passes over the whole picture: strands, melt and tear, molecules. It runs in this browser only; none of it has been on the RG DS Plus. The screen shaders follow the DS contract from the DSSHADE-001 guide; the page passes do not exist on the device.

Source: lab/web/rift.html, rift.js (the stage and the verbs), dshost.js (the DS contract), rift-a.js, rift-b.js, rift-c.js. Companions: the DS Shader Sketchbook and the Lattice Arcade.
