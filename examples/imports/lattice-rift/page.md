## What it is

Lattice Rift is an interactive WebGL instrument for melting, tearing and mending a lattice. Start with the included scene and change the controls to inspect how the rendered surface responds.

This experiment runs in your browser. It does not need a model request to produce its graphics.

## Why we made it

A fixed geometric structure can produce many different images when its deformation rules become interactive. This experiment keeps the controls beside the result so changes can be explored directly.
