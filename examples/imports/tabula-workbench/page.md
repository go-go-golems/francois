## What it is

Tabula Workbench is a programmable reactive workbook. Its sheets combine live inputs, calculations and charts. Change an input and inspect the resulting table or plot in the same workspace.

Workbook drafts live in this browser. The hosted version also supports model-assisted editing within the site's shared hosting limits.

## Why we made it

Calculations and their visual output are easier to inspect when they remain together. This experiment makes the workbook itself programmable, with immediate feedback from its inputs and charts.
