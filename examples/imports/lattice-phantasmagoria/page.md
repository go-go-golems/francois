## What it is

Lattice Phantasmagoria is a playable dual-screen canvas and WebGL experiment. Choose a cartridge or scene and use its controls to explore the output across the two displays.

The scenes run in your browser. This proof of concept does not require a hosted model request for its graphics.

## Why we made it

Two simultaneous displays allow a scene to expose different views and controls without replacing the main output. This experiment explores that arrangement through a collection of playable scenes.
